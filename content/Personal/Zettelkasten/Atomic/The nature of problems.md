### What are problems anyways? and *why* should you care about them?
*Problems are like pregnancy*. They will keep growing until you put an end to them. No one is just a little bit pregnant. Every problem needs to be dealt with. 

### The right way to see them
First of all, you must remember that every ducking person on this world has problems to deal with.  You're not suffering alone.

**Problems are like a window to a new horizon of opportunities.** Each problem that you solve will give you the tools to success. And always remember *if you can solve it for yourselves, you can also solve it for the millions of people who are also dealing with it and get paid for that*. 
Instead of seeing them as a obstructer, see them as a tool to build a path to success.  Don't let them control you, be the one who controls.
